Workload: 2000 round trips of a 1 KiB payload on one loopback TCP connection.
`echo` drives it with a bare `asyncio.Protocol` on each side. `stream_echo`
drives the same traffic through `asyncio.start_server()` and
`asyncio.open_connection()`, so the difference between the two rows is the cost
of the standard library's pure-Python streams layer on top of the transport.

At this baseline the streams layer adds 7.1 ms to zuvloop's median, 3.6 us per
round trip, or 23% over the protocol echo. That is the most a native stream
reader and writer could recover on this workload.
